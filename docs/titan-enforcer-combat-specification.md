# Titan Enforcer Unit-94 Combat Specification

## 1. Unit Overview
- **Designation:** Titan Enforcer Unit-94 (Auxiliary Nuclear Power)
- **Base HP:** 650 HP (Heavy Armored Core)
- **Damage Reduction:** 45% standard damage resistance
- **Location:** Sector E Containment Chamber ($X \in [30, 54], Z \in [0, 24]$)

---

## 2. Combat Phases
### Phase 1: Heavy Sentry Patrol ($\text{HP} > 30\%$)
- **Patrol Speed:** $1.8\text{ m/s}$
- **Chase Speed:** $2.7\text{ m/s}$
- **Attack:** Gatling cannon bursts ($7.5\text{ dmg/shot}, 0.22\text{s cadence}$)
- **Stomp Shockwave:** $0.95\text{s}$ interval screen rumble while moving.

### Phase 2: Enraged Overdrive ($\text{HP} \le 30\%$)
- **Armor Fractured Trigger:** Emissive core shifts to critical crimson.
- **Speed Multiplier:** $+35\%$ movement velocity.
- **Ground Slam Attack:** Seismic shockwave executed at $\approx 5\text{s}$ intervals.
- **Searchlight:** Focused beam with expanded detection arc.

---

## 3. Stagger & Vulnerability Mechanics
- Heavy damage ($\ge 35\text{ damage}$ from gas canisters or concentrated rifle fire) inflicts a $3.5\text{s}$ hydraulic recalibration stagger window.
- Defeat triggers dropping of the **Master Access Key** and blueprint map leading to the Secret Laboratory.
