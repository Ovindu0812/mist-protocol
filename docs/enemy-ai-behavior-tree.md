# 🧠 Enemy AI Systems - Behavior Trees, GOAP & Sensory Pipeline

## 2. Agent Archetypes

### 2.1 The Sentinel Automaton (`SentinelBrain.cs`)
- **Archetype**: Medium-range tactical infantry.
- **Decision Engine**: Goal-Oriented Action Planning (GOAP).
- **Available Actions**:
  - `PatrolWaypoints`: Traverses factory zones when unalerted.
  - `InvestigateSound`: Moves toward acoustic stimuli generated by running or gunshots.
  - `TakeCover`: Identifies tactical low-cost cover cells in `NavGrid` during sustained fire.
  - `FlankPlayer`: Coordinates with squad members via `SentinelSquad.cs` to encircle Nila.
  - `AttackRifle`: Fires burst-fire assault rifle volleys with line-of-sight checks.

### 2.2 The Hunter Drone (`HunterDroneBrain.cs`)
- **Archetype**: Aerial scout and searchlight interceptor.
- **Decision Engine**: 2D Grid Suspicion Map (`SuspicionMap.cs`).
- **Mechanics**:
  - Sweeps factory corridors with downward conical spotlights.
  - Accumulates suspicion levels $[0, 1]$ over time when player is partially obscured.
  - Emits high-frequency alarm chirps alerting nearby ground units upon full target confirmation.

### 2.3 The Saboteur (`SaboteurBrain.cs`)
- **Archetype**: Stealth ambusher and trap deployer.
- **Decision Engine**: Utility AI evaluating curves over distance, health, and player weapon state.

### 2.4 The Titan Automaton (`TitanBossBrain.cs`)
- **Archetype**: Heavy armored boss platform.
- **Decision Engine**: Multi-phase hierarchical state machine.
- **Special Behaviors**:
  - `Phase 1`: Patrols vault perimeter, tracks player with high-intensity searchlight, absorbs 55% bullet damage.
  - `Phase 2 Enraged`: Activates upon armor fracture (< 50% HP). 35% speed boost, crimson searchlight strobe, ground slam shockwaves.
