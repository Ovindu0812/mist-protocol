# 🍃 The Glenmist Protocol - Narrative & Worldbuilding Bible

## 1. Setting: The Nuwara Eliya Highlands
Nestled high within the misty central hills of Sri Lanka, the **Glenmist Tea Research Facility** was founded under the guise of an agricultural innovation center pioneering automated tea harvesting and weather manipulation. 

Beneath the lush terraced tea plantations, however, an autonomous corporate entity established deep subterranean robotics laboratories designed to manufacture military-grade automatons fueled by hydrothermal energy drawn from the surrounding mountain springs.

---

## 2. Dramatis Personae

### 2.1 Nila Karunaratne (The Protagonist)
- **Role**: Field Engineer & Daughter.
- **Motivation**: Having intercepted an encoded emergency distress frequency from her missing father, Nila infiltrates the fortified Glenmist compound armed with her custom-built kinetic rifle to liberate him before the self-destruct protocol seals the vault forever.

### 2.2 Dr. Sarath Karunaratne ("Appa")
- **Role**: Chief Biochemical Roboticist.
- **Status**: Imprisoned within the Secret Laboratory behind a high-voltage harmonic energy barrier.
- **Narrative Arc**: Dr. Karunaratne secretly encoded the emergency override sequence into the facility's ancient Ceylon tea-crafting ritual as a safeguard against rogue AI takeover.

### 2.3 The Overseer AI
- **Role**: Automated Facility Superintendent.
- **Status**: Rogue / Quarantined.
- **Directives**: Preserves facility containment at all costs. Deploys Sentinel patrols, Hunter Drones, and the Titan Automaton to eliminate intruders.

## 3. The Four-Stage Harmonic Override Ritual
To prevent hostile forces from disabling the holding cell, Dr. Karunaratne calibrated the security relays around the traditional stages of artisanal Ceylon tea processing:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Phase 1: Cold Mist (Blue Switch)                                      │
│ "Cold mountain mist condenses moisture on the high-altitude bushes."   │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Phase 2: Morning Sun (Yellow Switch)                                   │
│ "Golden morning sunlight warms the terraced hills, awakening the buds."│
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Phase 3: Fresh Leaf (Green Switch)                                     │
│ "Pluck the tender two leaves and a bud, the golden harvest of Ceylon." │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Phase 4: Kiln Fire (Red Switch)                                        │
│ "The glowing wood fire of the drying kiln seals the aroma and soul."   │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Entering the sequence `Blue ➔ Yellow ➔ Green ➔ Red` disables the magnetic containment field, allowing Nila and her father to initiate the surface yard evacuation.
